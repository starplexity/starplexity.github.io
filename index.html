Privacy Policy

Tic Tac Toe Puzzle
Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy describes the current offline Android release of Tic Tac Toe Puzzle (the “Game”), published by Starplexity. It applies to the Game, not to separate websites or services.

Contact

For privacy questions about the Game, email starplexity@gmail.com. If you choose to send an email, the information in your message is provided by you through your email service and is used by the developer to respond.

Information used by the Game

The Game does not require an account. This release has no online multiplayer, advertising, analytics, leaderboard, cloud service, or game server. The Game does not send player names, game activity, or audio preferences off the device.

Players may enter a name, including a real name or nickname. The Game uses the entered text to label players during play. It is held in the active game session, is not transmitted, and is not saved by the Game. Other people using a shared device may see the name on screen. A nickname can be used instead.

Music and sound-effect choices and volume levels are stored in Android app-local preferences so they can persist between sessions. They remain on the device until changed or the Game's local data is cleared. Android backup behavior, if enabled, is controlled by Android and the device owner's settings.

Data sharing and third-party services

The Game does not transmit the information described above to the developer or third parties. This release does not include advertising, analytics, account, social, online, or cloud-save services. Google Play and Android may process information under their own terms when you use their platform services; those practices are controlled by Google and Android.

Retention, security, and deletion

Player names are used during the active game session and are discarded when that session ends or the app process is cleared. Audio preferences remain in local app storage until changed or the app's local data is cleared. The Game has no server copy of this information. Device security and any operating-system backup are managed by the device and Android settings.

Children and families

The Game is designed for a general audience and may be used by children. It has no chat, user account, advertising, or online social features in this release. Names entered by players are displayed locally and are not transmitted by the Game. Parents and guardians may guide children to use a nickname on a shared device.

Changes to this policy

If a future release adds online features, advertising, analytics, accounts, or another data practice, this policy and the relevant disclosures will be updated before those features are released.
