Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy describes the first offline release of Tic Tac Toe : X and O Puzzle (the “Game”), published by Starplexity. It applies to the Game on Android and is intended for a general audience, including children.

INFORMATION USED BY THE GAME

The Game does not require an account and this release does not include online multiplayer, advertising, analytics, a leaderboard, cloud services, or a game server. The Game does not send information to the developer or to third parties.
Players may enter a name, including a real name or nickname. The Game uses that text only to label players on the device during play. It is held in the active game session and is not transmitted or saved by the Game. On a shared device, other people who can see the screen may see the entered name. Players can use a nickname or leave a name blank.
The Game stores music and sound-effects enabled choices and volume levels in Android app-local preferences so those choices can persist between sessions. They remain on the device until changed or the Game's local data is cleared. The Game does not upload these preferences. Android backup behavior, if enabled on a device, is controlled by Android and the device owner's settings.

DATA SHARING AND THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The Game does not collect or transmit the information described above and does not include advertising, analytics, account, social, online, or cloud-save SDKs in this release. Google Play and Android may process information under their own terms when you use their platform services; those practices are controlled by Google and Android, not by this Game.

RETENTION, SECURITY, AND DELETION

Player names are used in the active session and are discarded when that session ends or the app process is cleared. Audio preferences remain in local app storage until changed or the app's local data is cleared. The Game has no server copy of this information. Device security and any operating-system backup are managed by the device and Android settings.

CHILDREN AND FAMILIES

The Game is designed for a general audience and may be used by children. It has no chat, account, advertising, or online social features in this release. Names entered by players are displayed locally and are not transmitted by the Game. Parents and guardians may guide children to use a nickname on a shared device.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If a future release adds online features, advertising, analytics, accounts, or another data practice, this policy and the relevant disclosures will be updated before those features are released.
